CASE STUDY / 03

ZEWARLY SOCIAL
MEDIA AUTOMATION.

From product catalog to social media — one connected workflow instead of hours of repetitive manual posting.

Product-based social content used to need the same manual steps every time: select a product, collect its name, description, URL and image, write the copy, then prepare posts for each platform. This automation connects all of those stages inside n8n.

WooCommerce→Google Sheets→JavaScript→Limit→Product Image→Gemini AI→Facebook→Instagram

AI that writes for the product

Gemini receives the real product name, description and URL — it never invents product information — and returns a hook, product-focused copy, a call to action and hashtags.

Business value

Less repetitive work, consistent formatting, and a process that scales with a larger catalog while the Limit node controls how much is processed.

Real challenges solved

Meta app permissions, developer-role configuration, authentication and expired access tokens were diagnosed and fixed during development.
